About Training Zone

One hundred and fifty years of training, one set of values. Here is the story.

Our History

Training Zone have been the leader in all kinds of training that covers management and IT on Plongk Street, Cairsville for the last 150 years.

What started as a small lecture room above a printing shop in 1876 has grown into the most trusted training company in the region - but the core idea has not changed: teach people things that make a real difference, in person, face to face.

Today we still teach on Plongk Street, in the same building. The walls are a little more crowded with whiteboards and the coffee machine is a little more modern, but the commitment is the same as it was in 1876.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: help local residents build practical, employable skills.

We focus on local residents aged 18 to 45, with a particular focus on mature-age job seekers returning to study or switching careers. The website itself has one business target: grow our local customer base by at least 40% by making it easier for people to find us, understand what we teach, and book their first session.

We run two streams of training:

  • IT Training - operating systems, data structures, algorithms, information security, cryptography, databases, data mining.
  • Management Training - performance management, leadership, appraisals, and the people skills that turn good employees into great managers.

Our Founder

Training Zone was founded by Terrence Zonas in 1876, and he is still our CEO and spokesperson today. Yes, really.

Terrence is an American computer scientist and internet entrepreneur who co-founded Goggle, one of the world's most profitable internet companies. But his real passion - the thing he still spends most of his time on - is Training Zone, where he likes to teach people things that make a difference.

His most famous training is the world-renowned "Lecture Clash" session, which has helped thousands of students deal with the dreaded timetable collision. Realise it's up to you. Attend one, get the notes from the other, find a friend in the one you miss, and decide which one to skip based on what you can actually catch up on. Terrence still runs that session himself every intake.
