Welcome to Training Zone

For 150 years, Training Zone has been the leader in all kinds of training that covers management and IT on Plongk Street, Cairsville. We are a local, family-run company focused on helping residents aged 18 to 45 - especially mature-age job seekers - build the practical skills that employers actually want.

Whether you are returning to study, switching careers, or stepping up into a leadership role, our trainers and our 150 years of know-how are here to help you make it happen.

Our Training

We deliver two main streams of training, both taught by working professionals who actually use these skills in the field.

IT Training

Operating systems, data structures, algorithms, information security, cryptography, databases, data mining, and more. Whether you are starting from scratch or refreshing existing skills, our IT stream has a path for you.

Management Training

Performance management, leadership, difficult conversations, appraisals, and the soft skills that turn a good manager into a great one. Built on real workplace experience, not just theory.
