Our story

A trusted optical practice, caring for the community

For over 25 years our clinic has been a trusted provider of eye care services in the community. We pride ourselves on offering friendly, dependable and expert optical care from our practice at 21 Florence Avenue, Razole.

Our mission is to help every patient achieve clear, comfortable vision through personalised service and the latest technology in optometry — and our team is passionate about educating patients so they understand their eye health and have the guidance they need for ongoing care.

  • Compassion, accuracy and dedication in every appointment.
  • Patient education in plain language, without rushing your questions.
  • The latest technology in optometry used for every assessment.
