Our staff

Optometrists, dispenser and reception

Three qualified optometrists, an experienced optical dispenser and a receptionist who is your first point of contact when you visit.

Dr. Melissa Javali

OD — Paediatric & Family Optometry

Dr. Melissa Javali has over 15 years of clinical experience in family and paediatric eye care. After earning her Doctor of Optometry degree from a leading Australian optometry school, she completed additional professional development in myopia control and vision therapy.

Dr. Daniel Wilson

BOptom — Paediatric Optometrist

Dr. Daniel Wilson focuses on early detection of children’s vision problems, including short-sightedness, long-sightedness, eye-coordination issues and digital eye strain. His friendly, patient-centred approach helps children feel comfortable and relaxed during eye examinations.

Dr. Alan Koya

BOptom — Diagnostic Optometry

With more than three decades in professional practice, Dr. Alan Koya is widely respected for his expertise in diagnostic optometry, ocular disease detection and vision rehabilitation. He frequently collaborates with ophthalmologists and general practitioners to ensure holistic patient care, particularly for individuals with chronic health conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.

Sarah Ravie

Optical Dispenser & Lens Specialist

Sarah Ravie is a highly skilled optical dispenser with over 12 years of hands-on experience helping clients choose eyewear that blends comfort, functionality and personal style — from everyday frames to specialty lenses.

Emiliano Brooklin

Receptionist

As the first point of contact at Vcare Opticals, Emi sets a warm and welcoming tone for every patient who walks in. With professional experience in customer relations and medical reception, he ensures daily operations run smoothly and that every visitor receives prompt, respectful and helpful service.

How we work

Personalised service, every visit

Whether you are experiencing vision concerns, need a new set of frames, or simply want an eye check-up, our experienced staff are here to support you.

  • We listen first. You tell us what has changed and what is worrying you before any testing begins.
  • We explain the results. Plain language, no jargon, and time for your questions.
  • We plan the next step together. Whether that is a prescription update, a treatment plan or a referral.
