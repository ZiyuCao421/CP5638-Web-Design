How to book

Three simple steps

  1. Choose an appointment type. Select an eye test, consultation, technology assessment or follow-up from the online booking form.
  2. Choose your preferred time. Add your preferred date and time, then include any information that will help us prepare for your visit.
  3. We confirm your appointment. We will contact you with the next available time and explain what to expect at your visit.
