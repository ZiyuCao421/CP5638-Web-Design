Services

Eye care for the whole family

We offer routine care, advanced diagnostic technology and convenient consultation options for people needing an eye examination, prescription update or specialised treatment.

Photographs on this page are illustrative images of the type of equipment and care involved in optometry. They are not photographs taken in the Vcare Opticals clinic.

Service 01

Routine vision tests

A routine vision test is the starting point for everything we do. We assess how clearly you see, how your eyes focus and work together, and how your eye health is tracking — then talk you through the results.

  • Vision assessment covering clarity, depth perception and colour vision
  • Review of your current glasses or contact lens prescription
  • Update of prescriptions when your eyes have changed
  • Clear explanation of what we found and what we recommend
Book an eye test
Service 02

Prescription lenses, eyewear and sunglasses

Alongside prescription lenses we provide stylish eyewear, sunglasses and specialty lenses to suit both functional and fashionable needs. Sarah Ravie, our optical dispenser and lens specialist, helps clients choose eyewear that blends comfort, functionality and personal style.

  • Prescription glasses and sunglasses
  • Specialty lenses for specific prescriptions
  • Frame fitting advice, so your lenses sit correctly and comfortably
  • Guidance on looking after and maintaining your eyewear
Book a frames consultation
Service 03

Contact lens fittings

Contact lenses need more than a prescription. Our fitting pairs you with lenses suited to your eyes and gives you the practical instruction and advice to wear them comfortably.

  • Fitting for contact lenses suited to your eyes
  • Instruction on insertion and removal
  • Advice on wearing, cleaning and caring for your lenses
  • Ongoing support if something does not feel right
Book a contact lens fitting
Service 04

Dry eye treatment

Dry, gritty or tired eyes are common, particularly for people who spend long hours on screens. We assess the surface of the eye and discuss treatment options and everyday habits with you, so you know what you can do between visits.

  • Assessment of your symptoms and the surface of the eye
  • Discussion of treatment options available to you
  • Advice on screen habits, lighting and eye care routines
  • Review visits to check how your symptoms are settling
Book a dry eye assessment
Service 05

Advanced diagnostic screening

Using the latest technology in optometry we screen for conditions such as glaucoma, cataracts and macular degeneration — often before you notice any symptoms yourself, because early detection greatly improves treatment outcomes.

Dr. Alan Koya's diagnostic expertise means our screening results are interpreted carefully, and where a patient would benefit, we work alongside ophthalmologists and general practitioners to support holistic care — particularly for people with chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.

  • Glaucoma — checks for the early signs of raised pressure and optic nerve change
  • Cataracts — early lens changes that can affect vision quality
  • Macular degeneration — retinal imaging and central vision review
  • Retinal imaging — advanced imaging to analyse retinal health
Book diagnostic screening
